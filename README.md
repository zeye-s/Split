# Split
Made with HTML, CSS, and JS. This is a simple 2D platformer game without any difficult mechanics or parkour. Reach the light green platform on the top left of the gameboard in order to win the game. Both players need to make it to the green platform in order for the level to complete. **** Sometimes the platforms are missing. You need to touch the red squares on the map in order to spawn these platforms which are highlighted in orange. There are also portals that are highlighted in yellow. Touching the portal teleports you to the other portal. There are a total of 5 stages, with stage 0 being just the parkour. Press M to open the stage selector screen or click the next button on complete. Pressing Space and restart on complete restarts the current level. Player controls are indicated by the icons underneath the gameboard. Have fun!
